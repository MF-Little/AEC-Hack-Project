Visual Vibes · glazing study

Glass is expensive, long-lead and hard to change once installed, yet it is usually chosen from images and opinions. Visual Vibes adds behavioural evidence from the people who will use the space.

Visual Vibes · factory egress · walking distance on a 0.25 m grid, lighter is closer to an exit

A floor grid gives the walking distance from every point to the nearest exit, around racking and machines. Each run starts at one of the farthest open spots, facing a random direction, so nobody can memorise the way out.

Visual Vibes · factory egress

Each hazard is a switch on the start screen. Lit signs stay readable in the power cut; unlit and photoluminescent ones fade, and smoke thins every sign by distance, so the same layout can be tested under different conditions.

Factory egress · the signs

Every sign is judged on what people did

In view

On screen, facing you, nothing in between, and enough light and clear air for its type.

Readable

Within its rated distance: Z factor × sign height, 200 for lit signs and 100 otherwise.

Looked at

Held within 3° of the centre of view for a quarter of a second, view steady.

Followed

Within 4 s, moved the way it points, or towards its door.
